How we started

Measured by hand, since day one.

Amali Curtain Center began as a small home workshop in Horana, measuring and stitching curtains for neighbours who wanted something better than off-the-shelf. That same hands-on approach is still how we work today.

There's no showroom staff standing between you and the person making your curtains. You speak directly to the craftsman - from the first measurement to the final install, and every cleaning visit after.

It's a slower way of doing business, but it's why customers who found us years ago still call us first.

The Amali Difference

A curtain isn't finished the day it's installed.

Amali customers get ongoing cleaning and after-service - not a one-time sale. Here's what that looks like in practice.

Scheduled cleaning

We check in periodically to clean and refresh installed curtains, so they keep looking as good as the day they went up.

Free minor adjustments

Rod slipping, hem settling, a ring that's come loose - small fixes are handled without a callout fee.

Direct line to the craftsman

You keep the same WhatsApp contact from your first consultation through years of aftercare - no call centre, no new staff each time.

Honest repair advice

If something's worth repairing rather than replacing, we'll tell you - even if a replacement would earn us more.
